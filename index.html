Your bills, on the fridge.

Every bill becomes a slip under a magnet on your fridge door. A yellow flag when it’s due soon, a red magnet when it’s late, and a big blue PAID stamp when you’ve paid it.

How it works

Three steps, every month.

  1. 1

    Put your bills up

    Rent, power, water, internet, cards, loans, tuition, taxes. Pick what kind of bill it is: the same every time, different each time, installments, or one-off.

  2. 2

    Get reminded

    A heads-up before each bill is due, on the day, and again if it’s late. For bills that change, Fridgie asks you for the amount.

  3. 3

    Stamp it PAID

    Tap the slip, check the amount, and the stamp comes down. Changed your mind? Undo is right there.

At a glance

Every slip tells you where it stands.

  • Yellow flag Due today or in the next 7 days.
  • Red magnet Overdue, with how many days late.
  • PAID stamp Paid, until the next one comes close.
  • Progress bar Installments, like “8 of 24 paid”.

The notepad on the freezer door adds up the month: what’s due, what’s paid and what’s left.

Features

Made for real household bills.

Bills that change

Electricity and water are different every month. Fridgie estimates from your last three bills until you type the real amount.

Loans that count down

See how many payments are made, what’s left to pay, and when it ends.

Autopay

For direct debits, Fridgie marks the bill paid on its due date, so the fridge stays right without you.

Kitchen calendar

Slide along the wall to the calendar: due days circled in blue, red or yellow.

History

What you actually paid each month, by category, and how often you paid on time.

Widgets

Due soon on your Home Screen, free. Next bill, This month, a mini fridge and Lock Screen widgets with Pro.

Any currency

Each bill keeps its own currency; totals are converted into yours.

Private by design

No account, no bank connection, no tracking. Your bills stay on your phone.
