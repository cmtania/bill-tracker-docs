Bill tracker for iPhone

Your bills, on the fridge.

Every bill becomes a slip under a magnet on your fridge door. A yellow flag when it’s due soon, a red magnet when it’s late, and a big PAID stamp when you’ve paid it.

How it works

Three steps, every month.

  1. Put your bills up

    Rent, power, water, internet, cards, loans, insurance, tuition. Choose the kind: the same every time, changes each time, installments, or one-time.

  2. Get reminded

    A heads-up before each bill is due, a reminder on the day at the time you pick, and a nudge if it’s late.

  3. Stamp it PAID

    Fill in the receipt, press the stamp, and PAID comes down on the slip. Changed your mind? Undo is right there.

At a glance

Every slip tells you where it stands.

  • Yellow flagDue today or in the next 7 days.
  • Red magnetOverdue, with how many days late.
  • PAID stampPaid, for the rest of that month.
  • Progress barInstallments, like “9 of 24 paid”.

Pay from the receipt: the amount, the day you paid, and what you paid with. Then press the stamp.

Features

Made for real household bills.

Bills that change

Electricity and water are different every month. Fridgie estimates from your last three bills until you type the real amount.

Loans that count down

“9 of 24 paid”, what’s left to pay, and a celebration when it’s paid off.

Autopay

For direct debits, Fridgie marks the bill paid on its due date, so the fridge stays right without you.

Print or share

Tap any receipt to print it, send it to family, or save it as a picture.

Kitchen calendar

Due days circled by status, with the month’s due, paid and left.

A kitchen that keeps time

The window follows your day from sunrise to stars, the clock shows your time, and the light switch turns on dark mode.

Widgets

Due soon on your Home Screen, free. Next bill, This month, a mini fridge and Lock Screen widgets with Pro.

Any currency

Each bill keeps its own currency. Totals are converted into yours.

Private by design

No account, no bank connection, no tracking. Your bills stay on your phone.

Receipts

A receipt for every month.

Every payment you stamp lands on that month’s receipt, with the total and how much you paid on time. A By category receipt shows where the money went.

Tap a receipt to see it up close, then print it, send it to the family, or save it to Photos.
