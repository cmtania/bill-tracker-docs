Getting started

How do I add a bill?

Tap + at the top of the Fridge tab, or walk up to the fridge and tap a free spot (the dashed outlines). First choose what kind of bill it is, then a category, and fill in the name, amount, currency, how often it’s due and the next due date. Tap Save, and its slip goes up on the fridge.

What are the four kinds of bill?
  • Same every time: rent, an internet plan. The amount you enter is due every time.
  • Changes each time: electricity, water, credit cards. Type the real amount when the bill arrives; until then Fridgie shows an estimate (≈), the average of your last 3 bills.
  • Installments: loans and gadget plans. Enter each payment and how many there are; Fridgie counts down (“8 of 24 paid”) and shows what’s left. If you’ve already made some payments, set Already paid when you add it.
  • One-time: a tax payment, tuition, a repair. One due date.
How do I mark a bill paid?

Walk up to the fridge, tap the bill’s slip and tap Mark paid. Check the amount, the day you paid and what you paid with, then tap Mark paid: the PAID stamp comes down on the slip. Changed your mind? Tap Undo in the message that appears, or tap the slip again and choose Unpaid. You can also mark bills paid from the Calendar tab and the bell.

A bill isn’t owed this time. What do I do?

In the Mark paid sheet, tap Skip this one. That due date stops counting as due or late, and the next one carries on as usual.

Why does a slip say “ADD AMOUNT” or “ENTER AMOUNT”?

ADD AMOUNT is on bills you picked during setup: they don’t have an amount or a real due date yet, so they get no reminders. Tap the slip, then Details → Edit to fill them in. ENTER AMOUNT is on a bill that changes each time when this month’s amount isn’t in yet; type it when you mark it paid, or tap Save the amount, pay later.

How do I edit, take off or delete a bill?

Tap its slip, then Details. There you can Edit it, see every payment, and Take it off the fridge when it’s paid off or you no longer owe it. Taken-off bills keep their payments in History, and you can put them back from Settings → Taken off the fridge. To delete a bill completely, with its history, use Delete bill at the bottom of Edit.

What does Autopay do?

Turn on Autopay for a bill that’s paid automatically, like a direct debit. On its due date Fridgie marks it paid for you, so you don’t have to. If the debit failed, open the bill’s details and swipe the payment to mark it unpaid. Fridgie never pays anything itself: it only records the payment.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Fridgie. The Due soon widget is free: overdue bills first, then what’s due in the next 7 days. Fridgie Pro adds Next bill, This month, a Mini fridge with all your slips, and three Lock Screen widgets (touch and hold the Lock Screen, then Customize).

The fridge and the calendar

What do the slips’ colours mean?
  • A yellow flag on the edge: due today or in the next 7 days.
  • A red magnet and a curled corner: overdue, with how many days late.
  • A blue PAID stamp: paid (or skipped). It stays until the next due date comes close.
  • A green bar: installments, with how many are paid.
What’s on the notepad and the blue display?

On the free fridge, the notepad on the freezer door shows this month’s totals: Due, Paid and Left, and how many bills are overdue. On the Pro fridge, the blue display on the left door shows what’s left to pay this month, how many are due and late, and how many of the 20 spots you’re using. The sticky note lists what’s due in the next 7 days.

What happens when my fridge is full?

The free fridge holds 5 bills; its 6th spot shows what Pro adds. With Fridgie Pro, your kitchen gets a stainless side-by-side fridge with room for 20 bills. 20 is the most Fridgie holds: take a bill off the fridge to make room for another.

How do I get to the calendar?

Swipe sideways in the kitchen, or tap Calendar in the switch at the bottom, to slide along to the calendar on the wall. Tap it to open the Calendar tab, where due days are circled (blue paid, red late, yellow due soon) and you can tap any day to see its bills.

What does the light switch do?

Tap the light switch on the wall, left of the fridge, or use Settings → Kitchen lights. With the lights off, the kitchen goes dark and the whole app switches to dark mode. With the lights on, Fridgie follows your device’s Light or Dark setting again.

Can I see my bills as a list instead?

Yes. Turn on Settings → Show fridge as a list. The list also appears automatically when VoiceOver is on, and you can mark bills paid from it.

Reminders

When do reminders arrive?

By default, one day before each bill is due at 9:00 AM, and again on the due day. In Settings, choose Remind me on the day, 1, 2 or 3 days before, or a week before, and pick the time with At. Each bill can also have its own reminder, or none. If a bill is still unpaid after its due date, Fridgie reminds you the next day, after 3 days and after a week. Marking it paid stops its reminders.

My reminders don’t arrive. What can I do?

Check these, in order:

  • In iOS Settings → Notifications → Fridgie, Allow Notifications is on.
  • In Fridgie’s Settings, Reminders is on and the time is right.
  • The bill has an amount, and its own reminder isn’t turned off.
  • A Focus mode, such as Sleep, isn’t silencing notifications at that time.

Open Fridgie once after changing a setting, so it can schedule the next reminders. iOS lets an app keep 64 reminders waiting at a time, so Fridgie schedules the soonest ones and adds more each time you open it.

Money and currencies

I pay bills in more than one currency. How are totals shown?

Each bill keeps the amount and currency you entered. Totals (on the fridge, the calendar, Calendar and History) add everything up in your Main currency, which you choose in Settings. When other currencies are converted in, the total is marked ≈, because your bank’s own rate and fees may differ a little. Fridgie uses the European Central Bank’s daily reference rates, downloaded at most once a day and only when you have a bill in another currency.

How does History work?

History shows what you actually paid, month by month (by the day you paid), by category, your on-time rate, recent payments and loans you’ve paid off. The free plan shows the last 3 months; 1Y and All come with Fridgie Pro.

Can Fridgie pay my bills?

No. Fridgie never connects to your bank, cards or billers. It keeps track of what’s due and what you’ve paid, and reminds you. If you save a Pay link for a bill, it’s there to open the biller’s page yourself.

Fridgie Pro and purchases

What does Fridgie Pro include?

A stainless side-by-side fridge with room for 20 bills, full history, the Next bill, This month, Mini fridge and Lock Screen widgets, kitchen themes, calendar styles, Face ID lock and extra app icons. You can get Pro three ways: Pro Yearly, with a 7-day free trial; Pro Monthly; or Lifetime, a one-time purchase. All three include exactly the same features.

How does the free trial work?

Pro Yearly starts with 7 days free, once per Apple ID. If you don’t cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the yearly plan starts and Apple charges you. If you’ve already used your free week, the button says Subscribe instead. Pro Monthly and Lifetime have no free trial.

How do I cancel Pro Yearly or Pro Monthly?

In Fridgie, go to Settings and tap Manage subscription. You can also open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and Fridgie. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple handles all subscription billing.

What happens when Pro ends?

Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. After that, the kitchen goes back to the single-door fridge with your first 5 bills (the ones you added first), and their reminders. Newer bills are locked, not deleted: they come back as soon as you get Pro again. History goes back to the last 3 months, the kitchen theme goes back to Sky, and Face ID lock turns off.

I bought Pro but it isn’t active. What do I do?

Go to Settings in Fridgie and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. This also brings Pro to a new iPhone or iPad.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the Fridgie purchase.

Themes, icons and Face ID

How do I change the kitchen or the calendar?

Go to Settings → Pro features and choose Kitchen theme or Calendar style. Sky and Classic are free; the others come with Fridgie Pro. The light switch works with every theme.

How do I change the app icon?

With Fridgie Pro, go to Settings → Pro features → App icon and choose Classic, Midnight or Steel. iOS shows a short message to confirm the change.

How does Face ID lock work?

With Fridgie Pro, turn on Settings → Pro features → Face ID lock. Fridgie then locks whenever you leave the app, and asks for Face ID (or your passcode) when you come back. Your face data never leaves your iPhone; Fridgie only learns whether it matched.

How do I turn off the sounds?

Fridgie plays short sounds for the light switch, picking a slip and the PAID stamp. Turn them off in Settings → Sound effects. They also stay quiet in Silent Mode, and they never stop music you’re playing.

Your data

Where are my bills stored?

Only on your iPhone or iPad. Fridgie has no account, no server and no connection to your bank, so we never see what you track. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I move to a new iPhone or iPad?

Fridgie’s data is included in your regular iPhone or iPad backup. Restore your new device from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start, and your fridge comes along. Then tap Restore purchases in Settings to bring back Fridgie Pro.

Does Fridgie sync between devices?

Not yet. Each device keeps its own bills, because Fridgie has no account or server. Fridgie Pro does carry over: on the other device, tap Restore purchases in Settings, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a bill from Details → Edit → Delete bill. To remove everything at once, go to Settings → Danger Zone → Reset All Data, type CONFIRM and tap Reset Everything. That deletes every bill, payment and reminder, and shows the welcome screens again; your settings are kept. Deleting the app also erases everything it stored. Your Fridgie Pro purchase stays with your Apple ID.
