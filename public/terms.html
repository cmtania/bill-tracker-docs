4. What Fridgie does, and doesn’t do

Fridgie helps you keep track of bills you enter yourself. It is an organiser, not a payment or financial service:

  • It doesn’t connect to your bank, cards or billers, and it can’t pay, schedule or cancel anything. Marking a bill paid, including the automatic marking for bills you set to autopay, only records it in the app; it doesn’t mean the biller received a payment.
  • Amounts, due dates, totals, estimates and receipts are worked out from what you enter. Estimates for bills that change each time are averages of past amounts. They may differ from what you actually owe, for example because of new charges, taxes, fees, late fees or exchange rates. Always check your actual bill.
  • Totals that include more than one currency are converted into your main currency using daily reference exchange rates, and are approximate (marked ≈).
  • Nothing in the app is financial advice.
